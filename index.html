How it works

Five steps. The only thing a person does is click Approve or Reject.

STEP 1

Invoice arrives

A supplier emails a PDF invoice. The system checks the inbox every minute.

– invoices received
STEP 2

AI reads it

Google Gemini pulls out supplier, amounts, VAT, PO number and date. No typing.

– read by AI
STEP 3

Robot checks it

4 rules: is VAT 15%? Already received? Has a ZATCA QR code? Has a PO number?

– checked · – stopped
STEP 4

Manager approves

Clean invoices go to the right manager by amount, who clicks Approve or Reject in an email.

– decided · – waiting
STEP 5

Payment planned

Every Sunday, approved invoices are scheduled for Thursday and suppliers are told by email.

Words on this page

Quick meanings for non-finance readers.

VAT (15%)

Tax added to the price in Saudi Arabia. A 1,000 SAR service should show 150 SAR VAT. A wrong figure means a wrong invoice.

ZATCA QR code

The QR code the Saudi tax authority requires on e-invoices. No QR usually means the invoice isn't valid for tax.

PO number

Purchase Order: the company's approval to buy, issued before the invoice. No PO means nobody ordered it officially.

Duplicate

The same invoice sent twice. Paying it again is one of the most common ways companies lose money.

On hold

Stopped by the checks. It won't reach a manager or get paid until someone fixes it.

About this project

Built by Shahwaiz Sulehri, a finance executive in Riyadh, to show how AI automation can take routine accounts payable work off a finance team: reading invoices, catching errors, routing approvals and planning payments. The whole pipeline runs on 4 connected n8n workflows. All suppliers and amounts are fictional demo data.
