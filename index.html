How the automation works

Five automated steps. The only manual action is a manager clicking Approve or Reject in an email.

1. Capture

Supplier emails a PDF invoice. The inbox is checked every minute.

–received

2. AI extraction

Google Gemini reads supplier, amounts, VAT, PO and date from the PDF.

–extracted

3. Validation

Four controls: VAT at 15%, duplicate, ZATCA QR code, PO number.

–checked · –held

4. Approval

Routed by amount to Supervisor, Finance Manager or CFO. Reminders after 2 days.

–decided · –pending

5. Payment plan

Weekly run schedules approved invoices and emails suppliers and finance.

–scheduled

Definitions

Terms used on this page

VAT 15%

Saudi VAT. The VAT on an invoice must equal 15% of the net amount.

ZATCA QR code

QR code the Saudi tax authority requires on every e-invoice.

PO number

Purchase order: proof the purchase was authorised before the invoice.

Duplicate

The same invoice received twice. A common cause of double payment.

On hold

Failed a control. Not sent for approval and not paid until resolved.
